malla reddy engineering college and management sciences

Malla Reddy Engineering College & Management Sciences (MREM), located in Medchal, Hyderabad, is a private engineering and management institute established in 2008, affiliated with JNTU Hyderabad, and known for its focus on technical education and career development. Here's a more detailed overview: Key Features and Highlights: Affiliation: Affiliated with Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University (JNTU), Hyderabad. Location: Situated on the Hyderabad-Nagpur National Highway (Medchal Road). Infrastructure: Offers spacious classrooms and a library with free access. Courses: Offers B.E./B.Tech, M.E./M.Tech, and MBA/PGDM programs across various specializations. Specializations: Includes Computer Science Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Electronics & Communication Engineering, Electronics Engineering, Information Technology, Mechanical Engineering, and Structural Engineering. Accreditation: The courses offered at the institute are approved by AICTE.
