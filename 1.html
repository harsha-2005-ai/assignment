HOTEL RESERVATION AND CANCELLATIONS

To make or cancel a hotel reservation, review the hotel's cancellation policy, contact the hotel or online travel agency directly, and follow their specific procedures, including checking for cancellation deadlines, penalties, and refund eligibility. Hotel Reservations: Booking: You can typically book a hotel room through the hotel's website, an online travel agency (OTA) like Booking.com, Expedia, or MakeMyTrip, or by contacting the hotel directly. Confirmation: After booking, you should receive a confirmation email with details like reservation number, hotel contact information, and the cancellation policy.
